Create a presentation for an internal technical review with the Giga engineering team and our external vendor's developers. The audience is technical (backend, data engineers, a couple of product people), so skip the high level mission intro and go straight into the substance.

Topic: proposed redesign of the school connectivity data ingestion pipeline that feeds GigaMaps.

Context:
Today data arrives from three sources: government school registries (CSV/Excel uploads, very inconsistent), ISP APIs reporting connectivity speed, and the Giga Daily Check App measurements from schools. Ingestion runs as nightly batch jobs. Main problems: duplicate schools across sources, no versioning of school records, validation errors are discovered only after data is published on the map, and onboarding a new country takes about 6 weeks of manual work.

Proposal:
1. A unified school identity layer with a matching service (fuzzy match on name, coordinates, admin codes)
2. A validation step before publishing, with a review queue for country focal points
3. Versioned school records so we can track changes over time
4. Move ISP and Daily Check data to near real time streaming; keep registries as batch

Structure I want (12 slides):
1. Title
2. Current architecture (diagram)
3. Pain points with concrete examples
4. Goals and non goals
5. Proposed architecture (diagram)
6. School identity and matching logic
7. Validation and review flow
8. Versioning model
9. Streaming vs batch: what changes
10. Migration plan in 3 phases
11. Risks and open questions
12. Decisions needed from this meeting

Style: clean, minimal text per slide, use diagrams and tables where possible, no stock photos, no marketing language. Add speaker notes with the details that don't fit on the slides.
